Privacy

Your data, plainly

What this site collects, why, where it goes, and how to have it deleted. Last updated 5 November 2026.

Who runs this site

Campus Velocity is run by Justin Peterson. For anything about your data, email justin@campusvelocity.com.

What we collect

  • The Risk & Trust Check. Your 12 answers, your email, and your name and school if you give them. We use them to show your results, to follow up about them, and, if you work with us, as your school's starting point.
  • The newsletter. Only if you tick the box. Your email and first name go to our newsletter provider. Every issue has an unsubscribe link.
  • Booking a call. When you book a results call, the details you enter go to Google Calendar, which runs the booking page.
  • Emails you send us. Kept so we can reply and follow up.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

Where it is stored

  • Check answers and results: the Campus Velocity app, on Supabase servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Website hosting: Vercel. The check is processed in Frankfurt.
  • Newsletter: Kit (kit.com), in the United States.
  • Call bookings: Google Calendar.
  • Fonts: this site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.

Why we're allowed to use it

Your check results and follow-up: because you asked for them, and we have a legitimate interest in following up with schools that take the check. The newsletter: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. If your school becomes a client, the client agreement covers the data in the Campus Velocity app.

How long we keep it

We keep your check results so they can be your school's starting point if you work with us later. Ask us to delete them and we will. Newsletter details are removed when you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. Email justin@campusvelocity.com and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
