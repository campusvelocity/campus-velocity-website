How it works

  1. Your team answers by role. Short question sets across all six domains, each answered by the person who actually knows: operations, front office, IT, leadership.
  2. You share the evidence. Logs, contracts, procedures and reports that back up the answers. Everything goes through the Campus Velocity app, stored in the EU.
  3. I review it personally. Every answer is checked against its evidence. Where they don't match, that's a finding.
  4. You get the results and a readout call. Scores, findings and your 90-day roadmap, walked through with your leadership team.

What you get

  • Six-domain risk matrix. Every domain scored on evidence.
  • Three gap scores. What you assume versus what you can show, open loops that never get closed, and single points of failure: the things only one person knows how to do.
  • A data privacy score across all six domains.
  • Vendor contract and auto-renew review. Every contract, owner and renewal date in one place.
  • Parent trust and escalation protocol. Who handles what, in what order, and how fast.
  • Your 90-day roadmap. The fixes in order, each with an owner and a date, tracked in the Campus Velocity app.

A good fit if

  • You're a private or international school with dedicated administrative staff.
  • You're willing to share real operational data, not just impressions.
  • You want a plan, not a report that sits in a drawer.

Probably not yet if

  • There's no one besides the head of school to gather information.
  • You need advice on a specific safety incident. That needs your safety adviser.
