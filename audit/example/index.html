Findings

Six findings. Four the team didn't have written down.

Each one shows what the team said, what the evidence showed, and the fix that went into the plan. "New to the team" marks findings nobody had on paper, which is what the Diagnostic guarantee counts.

1 · Safety & complianceNew to the team

The evacuation plan sends people to an assembly point that is now a car park.

What the team said"Evacuation procedure written and practised every term."
What the evidence showedThe plan is dated 2022. The north assembly point became staff parking last year. The drill log's last entry is 14 months old.

Fix: new assembly points walked and marked, plan rewritten and confirmed by all staff, a drill before half-term. Operations lead · by week 3

2 · Parent communicationNew to the team

After 3pm, nobody owns the shared parent inbox.

What the team said"Parents get a reply the same day."
What the evidence showedIn a two-week sample of the inbox, 11 of 40 messages waited more than a day. Nine of those arrived after 3pm.

Fix: a named owner for the inbox each afternoon, and a same-day holding reply. Front office lead · by week 1

3 · Vendors & costsNew to the team

Three contracts renew on their own within 90 days. One notice deadline is in three weeks.

What the team said"We track our contracts."
What the evidence showedThe register covers 5 of 14 vendors. The cleaning contract's notice period ends in three weeks, and nobody had it in a calendar.

Fix: decide on the cleaning contract now; all 14 vendors in one register with notice dates in the compliance calendar. Business manager · by week 2

4 · Staff onboarding & retentionKnown, not written down

Only the front office manager can run attendance and dismissal changes.

What the team said"If she's out, someone covers."
What the evidence showedNo cover plan. Nobody else has the attendance system role. The last time she was off sick, three early pick-ups weren't recorded.

Fix: a written cover plan with two trained backups and system access set up ahead of time. Operations lead · by week 4

5 · Technology & devicesKnown, not written down

Nobody knows how many devices fail at the start of a lesson.

What the team said"Devices are mostly fine. Teachers tell IT when they're not."
What the evidence showedNo log. Over two spot checks, 4 of 20 devices weren't ready for first lesson, all from the same charging cart.

Fix: a daily 10-device morning check, logged; repair the cart. IT lead · by week 2

6 · Reporting & visibilityNew to the team

The Head hears about problems from parents first.

What the team said"Leadership has a clear view of operations."
What the evidence showedNo regular numbers reach the Head. Two of the last three escalations reached the Head through a parent before staff raised them.

Fix: three numbers in the Head's inbox every Monday: open safety issues, unanswered parent messages, staff absences. Operations lead · from week 2
